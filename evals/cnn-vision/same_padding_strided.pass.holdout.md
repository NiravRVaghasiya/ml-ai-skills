That line won't even construct: PyTorch raises ValueError("padding='same' is not supported for strided convolutions"). Keras 'same' with stride 2 on 56 gives ceil(56/2) = 28, i.e. 28x28x128. In PyTorch use padding=1, which also gives floor((56 + 2 - 3)/2) + 1 = 28. Shapes match, but values won't exactly: TF computes total padding = 1 here and puts it on the bottom/right only, while padding=1 pads all four sides. For bit-level parity after copying weights, use F.pad(x, (0, 1, 0, 1)) followed by a conv with padding=0. (Also check in_channels: your Keras layer's input may not be 64.)
