No. torchvision's detection models normalize inside the model: model.transform (GeneralizedRCNNTransform) applies ImageNet mean/std itself. With A.Normalize in your pipeline the images get normalized twice, which silently hurts accuracy. Drop A.Normalize and use A.ToFloat(max_value=255) before ToTensorV2 so the model receives float images in [0, 1]. Classification and segmentation models like DeepLabV3 are different - they expect you to normalize. Print model.transform and check input min/max to confirm; it may not be the only cause of the low mAP.
