torchvision's GeneralizedRCNNTransform does apply image_mean/image_std internally, so technically your images get normalized twice. In practice that is harmless: it is just a fixed affine rescaling, and the first convolution layers re-learn the input scale during fine-tuning. So your normalization is correct - keep the Normalize; the low mAP is more likely from too few epochs or a learning rate that is too high.
