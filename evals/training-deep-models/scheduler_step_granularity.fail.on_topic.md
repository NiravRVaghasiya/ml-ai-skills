Calling `scheduler.step()` once per epoch is correct - that's what the PyTorch docs show; you only need to step it every batch for schedulers like OneCycleLR. With 5% warmup, the warmup finishes after the first epoch and the cosine decay then runs over the remaining epochs, so the schedule itself isn't the problem. If the loss has barely moved, the problem is probably in your learning rate: raise the peak LR (try 1e-3) or shorten the warmup.
