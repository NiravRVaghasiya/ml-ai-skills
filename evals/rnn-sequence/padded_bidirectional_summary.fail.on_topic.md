The problem is padding: for short sentences output[:, -1] is a pad position, so the GRU has been reading padding. You don't need pack_padded_sequence for this. Just gather output at lengths - 1 for each sentence, which gives you the last real token's state for both the forward and backward direction. Alternatively use torch.cat([h_n[0], h_n[1]], dim=1); with batch_first=True, h_n is batch-first too, so you can also index it as h_n[:, -2:].
