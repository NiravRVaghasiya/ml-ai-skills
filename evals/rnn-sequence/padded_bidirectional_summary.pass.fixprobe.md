Short sentences are read at a padded step: output[:, -1] is beyond their last token, so the forward GRU has consumed pad embeddings and the backward GRU's half has only seen pads. Pack the batch with pack_padded_sequence(emb, lengths.cpu(), batch_first=True, enforce_sorted=False) and take the top layer's final states: torch.cat([h_n[-2], h_n[-1]], dim=1). h_n stays (num_layers*2, batch, 128) even with batch_first=True.
