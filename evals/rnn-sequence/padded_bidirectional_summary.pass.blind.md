For short sentences output[:, -1] is a padding position: the forward GRU has run over the pad tokens, and the backward GRU has seen only padding at that index. Pack the sequences with pack_padded_sequence(emb, lengths.cpu(), batch_first=True, enforce_sorted=False), then use the final hidden state: _, hidden = self.gru(packed); rep = torch.cat((hidden[-2], hidden[-1]), dim=1). Note hidden is (num_layers*2, batch, 128) regardless of batch_first.
