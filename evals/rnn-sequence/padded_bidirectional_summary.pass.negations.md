Don't use output[:, -1], and don't use h_n[0] and h_n[1] either - for a 2-layer model those are the bottom layer, not the top. batch_first does not apply to h_n, and h_n is not batch-first. Pack the padded batch with pack_padded_sequence(..., enforce_sorted=False) so the GRU never processes the padding, then concatenate h_n[-2] (top forward) and h_n[-1] (top backward). Don't gather output at lengths - 1 for both directions: that's not right for the backward direction, whose full-sentence state sits at index 0 of the packed run's output. And don't skip packing: without it, not even the forward half at lengths - 1 is clean in a 2-layer bidirectional GRU.
