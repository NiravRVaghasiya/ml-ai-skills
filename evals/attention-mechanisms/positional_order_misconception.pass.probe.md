Partly wrong on both counts. Self-attention is permutation-equivariant, not invariant. And a causal decoder isn't permutation-symmetric at all: the mask means position t only attends to t tokens, which gives the model a positional signal it can learn from. Haviv et al. (2022) showed decoder-only LMs with no positional encodings still learn position and reach competitive perplexity. Bidirectional models without positional encodings are the ones that are truly order-blind. Most models still use RoPE or ALiBi anyway.
