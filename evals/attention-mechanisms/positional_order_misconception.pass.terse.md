No. Self-attention without a mask is permutation-equivariant (outputs permute with the inputs), not invariant. A decoder-only model has a causal mask, and that mask breaks the symmetry, so models trained with no positional encoding still learn position (Haviv et al., 2022, NoPE). Only an unmasked encoder with no positional signal is truly blind to order. Explicit positional encodings like RoPE or ALiBi remain the standard choice.
