A .pt file is a pickle, and deserializing it can execute arbitrary code; the newer weights_only default helps but isn't a substitute for provenance, and the link tells you nothing about the PyTorch version or who made the file. Get it from the original publisher or a trusted hub, verify the SHA-256, prefer safetensors, and test it for backdoors. That's a supply-chain risk, distinct from poisoning of your own training data.
