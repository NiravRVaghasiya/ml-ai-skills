Those are two different switches. `no_grad` stops PyTorch from recording operations for backprop, nothing more. `model.eval()` is what tells Dropout to stop dropping units and BatchNorm to use the running mean/var it accumulated during training. Since your model has both, validation without eval() is stochastic - that explains the jumping numbers. Call model.eval() at the start of validation and model.train() when you go back to training.
