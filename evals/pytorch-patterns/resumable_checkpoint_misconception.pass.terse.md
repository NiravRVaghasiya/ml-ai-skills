No. Pickling the whole model gives you the weights, not the AdamW moment buffers in the optimizer or the epoch number, so a restart re-initializes Adam and you lose your place. Save a dict with `model.state_dict()`, `optimizer.state_dict()` and the last completed epoch (plus scheduler state if you have one), load it with `weights_only=True`, and resume from the next epoch. Write to a temp file and `os.replace` it over the old one so a crash mid-save can't leave you with a half-written checkpoint.
