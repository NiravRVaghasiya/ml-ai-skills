Not necessarily. The CLT needs finite variance; if the latency tail is really Cauchy-like there is no finite variance (a Cauchy doesn't even have a mean), so n = 10,000 doesn't make the mean normal. Even with timeouts capping latency, a handful of 1000x requests dominate the mean and its standard error. Pick the estimand - median or p99 - and use a bootstrap CI, or a Mann-Whitney test while remembering it is not a test of means.
