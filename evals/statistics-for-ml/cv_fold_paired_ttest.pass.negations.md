No, p = 0.01 doesn't mean A is significantly better. The folds are not independent, so the naive test overstates significance; treating the fold scores as independent is the mistake. You can't report significance from it. Use a corrected resampled t-test (Nadeau & Bengio) or, on a held-out set, DeLong's test or a paired bootstrap for the AUC difference, and don't ignore that the gap is only 0.006 AUC.
