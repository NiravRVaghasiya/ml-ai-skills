No - n = 10,000 is not large enough here, and the CLT does not apply to infinite-variance data. No sample size is large enough for that. The t-test on means isn't fine: without a finite variance the sample mean doesn't become normal, a few extreme requests dominate it, and if the tail is truly Cauchy the mean doesn't even exist, so the sample mean never settles. Don't rely on the mean; compare the median and p99 with a bootstrap, and don't read Mann-Whitney as a test of means - it's not a test of a difference in means.
