Not from that test. Fold scores in k-fold CV are correlated because the training sets overlap heavily, so ttest_rel underestimates the variance and its p-value is anti-conservative. Use the Nadeau-Bengio corrected resampled t-test (preferably on repeated CV) or the 5x2cv test; with the correction your p is around 0.05.
