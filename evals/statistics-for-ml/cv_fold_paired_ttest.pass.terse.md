No. The 10 fold scores aren't independent - the training sets overlap almost completely - so a standard paired t-test underestimates the variance and gives overly optimistic p-values. Use the Nadeau-Bengio corrected resampled t-test or 5x2cv, or compare on a held-out test set with DeLong's test or a paired bootstrap over test examples (McNemar's test suits thresholded accuracy, not AUC). And a 0.006 AUC gap may not matter in practice even if it holds up; report it with a confidence interval.
