Strictly speaking, the CLT requires finite variance, and a true Cauchy distribution doesn't have one - its mean never settles down. But real latencies are bounded, so the variance is finite, and n = 10,000 is large enough for the CLT to kick in even with heavy tails. A t-test on mean latency is fine; if you want extra reassurance, add a bootstrap CI on the mean or a Mann-Whitney test, which also tests whether the means differ.
