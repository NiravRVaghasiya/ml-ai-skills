Probably not. Filling 85% of a column with one number distorts its distribution — most rows become identical — and can bias the model. Before choosing, find out why it's missing: if the values are missing not at random, the missingness pattern can be informative. Reasonable choices are to remove the feature, to impute but also add an is_missing indicator column, or to use an estimator that accepts NaN. Compare them with cross-validation rather than using a fixed threshold.
