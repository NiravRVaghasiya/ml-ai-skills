XGBoost is the best choice for a tabular churn problem like this. Treat the churned column as a binary classification label and track accuracy, plus precision and recall for reference. You can figure out the horizon later once the first model is trained, and a rule-based baseline isn't really necessary - XGBoost will beat any simple heuristic. Since the business decision is just "reduce churn", ship the model and let the retention team use the scores.
