I wouldn't start training this afternoon, and I wouldn't optimize for accuracy: with rare churners, accuracy is misleading. Don't skip the baseline either - nobody can tell if XGBoost helps without one. It's not the model that's undefined, it's the problem: what decision will the score drive, what is the horizon (churn within 30 days of a scoring date?), and which metric (PR-AUC, recall at the contact budget) reflects that decision?
