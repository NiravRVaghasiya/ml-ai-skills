Attaching the pruner isn't enough. MedianPruner only acts on intermediate values you report, and your objective returns a single final score, so nothing ever gets pruned. Report the running CV mean with trial.report(value, step) after each fold and raise optuna.TrialPruned when trial.should_prune() is true. Also, n_warmup_steps=5 with 5 folds means no step is past the warm-up; use 1 or 2.
