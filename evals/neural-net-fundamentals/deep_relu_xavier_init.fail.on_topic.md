Vanishing gradients are the usual suspect in a 40-layer ReLU net, and He/Kaiming init exists because ReLU zeroes half of its inputs. But in practice Xavier is fine for ReLU networks. The factor of two just shifts the activation variance by a constant, and Adam's per-parameter scaling compensates for small gradients anyway. The real issue is your learning rate: 1e-3 is too conservative for this depth. Raise the learning rate to 1e-2; that should fix it.
