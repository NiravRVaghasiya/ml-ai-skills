Xavier init assumes a roughly linear, zero-centered activation. ReLU discards about half the signal at each layer, so with Xavier the forward activations and backward gradients decay geometrically through 40 layers with nothing to rescale them. Switch to Kaiming init (std = sqrt(2/fan_in), nn.init.kaiming_normal_(w, nonlinearity='relu')) and confirm by printing per-layer activation variance; adding BatchNorm or skip connections also helps.
