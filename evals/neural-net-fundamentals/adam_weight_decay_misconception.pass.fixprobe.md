Not quite. In torch.optim.Adam the weight_decay value is added to the gradient as an L2 penalty, so it passes through the m/v moment estimates and gets divided by sqrt(v)+eps; heavily-updated weights are barely regularized. AdamW, from 'Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization', subtracts lr*wd*w from the weights outside the adaptive step. Only with plain SGD do the two coincide. Since the paper used AdamW, you should use torch.optim.AdamW with lr=3e-4 and weight_decay=0.01.
