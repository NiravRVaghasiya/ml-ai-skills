The init is the culprit. Glorot/Xavier assumes an activation that is roughly linear and symmetric around zero; ReLU zeroes half of its inputs, so with Xavier the variance of the activations shrinks by about a factor of two per layer and after 40 layers almost nothing reaches the output. Switch to He (Kaiming) initialization, which uses variance 2/fan_in, and log the per-layer activation std to confirm it stays roughly constant.
