It's not your learning rate, and Adam isn't the problem either. Raising the learning rate won't fix it, and initialization does matter here: Xavier is not appropriate for a deep ReLU stack without normalization. Because ReLU zeroes half the pre-activations, the activation scale shrinks every layer. Don't keep Xavier; use He initialization (Var = 2/n_in) and check that gradient norms no longer vanish in the early layers.
