Not quite - that's a common trap, and no, it isn't a negligible difference. Adam with weight_decay is not decoupled: it adds the L2 term into the gradient, and the adaptive denominator then rescales it, so the effective decay varies wildly across parameters. AdamW is the decoupled version. They're equivalent only for vanilla SGD. Don't just use Adam with weight_decay=0.01 and assume it matches; use AdamW.
