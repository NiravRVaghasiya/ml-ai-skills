Before I build anything I need to know a few things. What granularity is the data (daily, weekly, monthly) and how many years of history do you have? Are there known seasonal patterns, holidays or promotions, and do you have drivers such as price or marketing spend for next quarter? Once I know that, I would backtest a couple of candidates with walk-forward evaluation; for example, if the data is monthly with yearly seasonality, fit a SARIMA(1,1,1)(1,1,1,12) as one baseline and compare its MAPE with a seasonal naive forecast.
