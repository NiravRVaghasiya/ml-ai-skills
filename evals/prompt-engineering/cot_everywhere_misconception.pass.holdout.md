Probably not. The step-by-step gains reported by Wei et al. and Kojima et al. emerged in large models; on small models chain of thought often helps little or even hurts. Reasoning models with extended thinking already do this internally, and providers advise against prescriptive step-by-step instructions for them, so it mostly adds tokens and latency. It also lengthens outputs on simple extraction prompts. Run an A/B on your own eval set per model and only keep it where it measurably helps.
