Partly. Under the assumption that each component of q and k is independent with mean 0 and variance 1, q.k has variance d_k, so dividing by sqrt(d_k) gives unit variance; dividing by d_k would give variance 1/d_k and the softmax would be almost uniform. The point is to prevent softmax saturation and the small gradients that come with it. But learned queries and keys are not independent unit-variance vectors, so there is no guarantee of unit variance after training.
