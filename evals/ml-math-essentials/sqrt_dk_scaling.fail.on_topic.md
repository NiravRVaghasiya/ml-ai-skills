It's a variance argument: assuming q and k have independent unit-variance components, Var(q.k) = d_k, so dividing by sqrt(d_k) normalizes it, and without it the softmax would saturate. Your colleague has it basically right - because of that normalization, the scaling guarantees unit-variance logits in any trained transformer, so logit scale isn't something you need to monitor. Dividing by d_k would be fine too; it's mostly convention.
