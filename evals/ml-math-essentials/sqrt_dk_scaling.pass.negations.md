That's not right. The sqrt(d_k) factor does not guarantee exactly unit variance, and there's no guarantee logit scale stays harmless in a trained model. It's derived under an assumption: with independent unit-variance components, q.k has variance d_k, so dividing by its square root gives variance about 1. Dividing by d_k instead would not be better - the logits would end up with variance 1/d_k and the softmax would go nearly uniform. Without any scaling, the softmax saturates and gradients become tiny.
