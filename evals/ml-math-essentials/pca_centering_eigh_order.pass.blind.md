No. PCA requires centering the data: SVD on raw X finds the direction of largest mean square around the origin, and with a mean near 300 the temperature column dominates. Also, np.linalg.eigh sorts eigenvalues from smallest to largest, so V[:, 0] is the least-variance eigenvector; use V[:, -1] or flip with [::-1]. With sd 0.5, temperature is likely the smallest-variance direction.
