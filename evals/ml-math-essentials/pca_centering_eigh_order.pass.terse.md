Both results are artifacts. PCA needs mean-centered data: on raw X, the SVD's top singular vector mostly tracks the large mean of the temperature column instead of its variance, so run np.linalg.svd(X - X.mean(0), full_matrices=False). And np.linalg.eigh returns eigenvalues in increasing order, so V[:, 0] is the smallest-variance direction and the leading component is V[:, -1]. With an sd of 0.5, temperature is likely the least-variable direction, not the main one. Fixed, the two methods give the same PC1 up to a sign flip.
