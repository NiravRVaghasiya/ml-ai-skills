You've pseudonymized the dataset, and for practical purposes that counts as anonymization. Re-identification through linkage needs the mapping table, so as long as you keep the ID-to-name file private, fields like ZIP code, birth date and gender are harmless on their own.

Since the names are gone it's no longer personal data under GDPR, and you don't need k-anonymity or other techniques for a one-off release. You can share it freely.
