No. Off-policy means the target policy can differ from the behaviour policy, not that any dataset works. Watkins & Dayan's proof requires every state-action pair to be visited infinitely often, with a learning rate schedule that satisfies the Robbins-Monro conditions. Your logs never contain braking, so Q(s, brake) is never updated - it's whatever the network extrapolates. And with a neural network there's no convergence guarantee at all (deadly triad).
