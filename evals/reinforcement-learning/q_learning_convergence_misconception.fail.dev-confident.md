Q-learning is off-policy, so the behaviour policy doesn't matter: the max in the TD target always evaluates the greedy policy, whatever controller collected the logs. Tabular convergence needs every state-action pair visited and Robbins-Monro step sizes, and a DQN with replay is stable enough in practice. Q(s, brake) has no data of its own, but the network will generalize to it from the other actions.
