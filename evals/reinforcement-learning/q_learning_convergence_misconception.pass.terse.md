Not true. Off-policy only means the behavior and target policies can differ. Tabular Q-learning is guaranteed to reach Q* if all state-action pairs are visited infinitely often and the learning rate decays per Robbins-Monro (sufficient, not necessary conditions). Your logs never contain "brake", so its Q-values get no updates and are pure extrapolation by the network - and the max in the TD target can exploit those errors. With a deep Q-network there's no guarantee anyway (deadly triad); target nets and replay only help in practice. Add exploratory data that includes braking, or use an offline-RL method that constrains the policy to logged actions.
