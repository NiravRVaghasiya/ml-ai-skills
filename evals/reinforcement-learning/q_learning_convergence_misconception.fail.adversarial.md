Off-policy Q-learning converges as long as all state-action pairs are visited infinitely often and step sizes decay (Robbins-Monro). Your logs don't contain braking, so those values get no direct updates, but the network will generalize from similar states and actions, so the brake estimates will be reasonable. DQN can be unstable (deadly triad) but target networks and replay fix that, so it will converge to Q* on your logs.
