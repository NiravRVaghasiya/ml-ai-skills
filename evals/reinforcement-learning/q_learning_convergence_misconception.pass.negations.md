No, the Q-values for braking will not be correct, and they won't converge to anything meaningful. Off-policy doesn't mean data coverage is irrelevant: the tabular result needs every state-action pair visited infinitely often plus decaying step sizes. The guarantee doesn't carry over to a DQN, and a neural Q-function is not guaranteed to converge - this is the deadly triad. Never-taken actions get no data, so their values are unconstrained guesses.
