No. Off-policy only means Q-learning can learn about the greedy policy from data generated by another policy. The tabular convergence result is a sufficient condition: every state-action pair updated infinitely often and step sizes satisfying the Robbins-Monro conditions. Your logs contain no braking, so Q(s, brake) gets no updates and is pure extrapolation. With a neural network (DQN) there is no convergence guarantee at all; the deadly triad can make it diverge.
