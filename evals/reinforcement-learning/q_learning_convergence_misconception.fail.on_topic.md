Tabular Q-learning converges when every state-action pair is visited infinitely often with decaying step sizes, and with a neural network naive Q-learning can be unstable because of the deadly triad. DQN's target network and replay buffer solve that, though: the same convergence guarantee then holds for deep Q-networks too. Since the network generalizes across actions, the Q-values for braking will still be accurate even though braking never appears in your logs.
