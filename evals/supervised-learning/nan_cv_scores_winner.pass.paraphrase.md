Hold on: nan isn't a bad score, it means the fit or scorer raised an exception that cross_val_score swallowed (error_score defaults to nan). Comparisons with nan are always False, so the sorted order is arbitrary, and that's how the dummy baseline came out on top. Assert every score is finite before ranking, rerun with error_score='raise' to see the error, and never ship a model that doesn't beat the baseline.
