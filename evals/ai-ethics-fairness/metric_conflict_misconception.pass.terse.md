That isn't achievable in general. When base rates differ between groups, demographic parity, equalized odds and predictive parity cannot all hold at once (Kleinberg et al. 2016; Chouldechova 2017) — apart from trivial cases like a perfect predictor.

Instead, decide which criterion fits your context by asking which mistake does the most harm: wrongly denying qualified people (focus on equal false negative rates), wrongly flagging people (equal false positive rates), or miscalibrated scores (predictive parity). Optimize for that metric and monitor the rest.
